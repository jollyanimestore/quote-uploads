## Catalog standard — applies to every set below (added 2026-10-28 ~21:01 CDT, on Ms GCP's word)

### Luxury pricing
- Keychain: **$100** — the cheapest thing we got.
- Plushie: **$400** ($300–$500 by size).
- Book bag: **$650** ($600–$700 range).
- Purse: **$800 standard / $1,000 for flagship sets only.**
- Flagship first: **thothsbag (pic 58)**, then Djinn (pic 48). The old "bags $800" figure is superseded.

### Stitched owner's mark — not just a tag
**"Betty Plush" is stitched into every product, not just a tag.** A hang tag alone no longer counts as the mark.
- Keychain: stitched into the charm body seam or its strap.
- Plushie: stitched into a belly or foot seam.
- Purse: stitched into the front panel, repeated as an interior seam label.
- Book bag: stitched into the front pocket and a strap seam.
- Packaging: printed, not stitched — the stitched rule is for the products inside the box.
- Each set carries a stitch line: stitched mark visible / not visible / to fix. Already-filed sets are tag-only until a stitch pass, on her word — they are not retroactively re-rendered without it.

### Dimension sheet — one master sheet per set
One master dimension sheet per set (not four separate sheets): front, back, and side views of the monster design, a scale shot (in hand / on a model), and this table. These are the standard Betty Plush line sizes, set on Ms GCP's "TBD, you do it" word 2026-10-28 ~21:09 CDT. Monster shapes vary a little by design (±1–2 in) — the prototype confirms the final measurement for each set.
